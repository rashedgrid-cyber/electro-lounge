Privacy policy

A social lounge for electrical engineers. This policy explains how information is handled by our public pages, publishing tools, and any authorized account connection.

Effective and last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the public privacy-policy page and the Electro Lounge content-publishing integration. The main website is being updated; additional services will be covered by updated disclosures before they are enabled.

2. Current services and information

Our publishing tools use publicly available RSS articles to prepare original electrical engineering posts and artwork, and publish approved content to our public RSS feed. This privacy-policy page has no registration, payment, or contact form.

  • Public publishing information: Article titles, source URLs, summaries, prepared posts, images, publication times, and workflow status records are used to operate and troubleshoot the publishing service. Published material is publicly accessible.
  • Hosting information: GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and access logs, under its own privacy statement.

3. Planned LinkedIn connection

The official LinkedIn API integration is not connected at the date of this policy. Linking to our public LinkedIn Page does not authorize account access.

If the official integration is approved and an authorized administrator chooses to connect an account, it will use LinkedIn OAuth authorization. Depending on the permissions approved by LinkedIn and granted by that administrator, the integration may receive account identifiers and basic account information, Page identifiers and administrative roles, access tokens, and information about posts created through the integration.

This information will be used to verify authorization, identify the permitted Page, publish approved posts and images, and record whether publication succeeded. We will not request LinkedIn passwords, collect private messages or contact lists, or use LinkedIn member data to train AI models. Any additional use or permission will require an updated disclosure and appropriate authorization before it is enabled.

4. Service providers and public sharing

We use n8n to run the publishing workflow, GitHub to store public content and host these pages, and OpenAI to help prepare and check drafts from public RSS source material. LinkedIn account tokens and private account information are not part of the RSS drafting inputs.

Public posts, images, source attribution, and feed entries can be viewed and copied by others. If LinkedIn publishing is authorized in the future, submitted content will be processed by LinkedIn and displayed according to the publication settings. Providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

Provider policies: GitHub privacy statement, OpenAI privacy policy, and LinkedIn privacy policy.

5. Cookies and analytics

This privacy-policy page does not set application cookies or include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels. GitHub and external services may process technical information or use cookies when you visit their services. Their own policies apply to those activities.

6. Retention and security

Operational records are kept for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. Public content remains available until it is removed from its publishing platform.

If a LinkedIn connection is enabled, authorization information will be kept in protected integration credentials and will not be published in the public website or repository. LinkedIn-derived data will be retained and deleted according to the approved purpose, LinkedIn's applicable API terms and retention requirements, and applicable law. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

If a LinkedIn connection is enabled, you can revoke its authorization through LinkedIn's permitted-services settings. Revocation will stop future authorized API use for that account. Removing local records does not automatically remove copies already published or retained by independent platforms.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our services or data practices change, including before activating an integration that introduces new data use. The date above identifies the latest version.
