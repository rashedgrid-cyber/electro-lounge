Industry Navigator 2026 Kicks Off in Vienna, Spotlighting Key T&D Sector Trends

Sun, 11 Oct 2026 09:32:24 GMT

🤝 Vienna, Austria.

Industry Navigator 2026 in Vienna is bringing professionals together for three days of discussion about supply chains, sector growth and the challenges facing power delivery.

Transmission moves electricity across the wider network, while distribution brings it closer to the people and businesses that use it. Both depend on equipment, materials and well-coordinated projects, so supply-chain discussions are relevant to the engineers planning and delivering the work.

The event combines business and technical topics. For our community, it is an opportunity to think about how industry changes connect with everyday decisions: equipment selection, project planning and the technology used to deliver power.

🔎 In practical terms, supply-chain planning means matching equipment lead times to the construction schedule. Transformers, switchgear and protection equipment can have different delivery and testing requirements. A useful technical programme explains those requirements, examines new technology and distinguishes measured performance from marketing claims. For beginners, the key lesson is that a workable power project needs both a sound electrical design and equipment that arrives, is tested and is integrated at the right time.
