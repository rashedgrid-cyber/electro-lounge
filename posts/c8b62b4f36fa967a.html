Pure DC Installs Final 75 MVA Transformer at North London Data Centre Campus

Fri, 16 Oct 2026 08:14:08 GMT

In the United Kingdom, Pure DC has completed the installation of the final LON01 power transformer at its 90 megawatt (MW) North London campus. The project involved positioning a 75 megavolt-ampere (MVA) Hitachi transformer using a gantry system with a capacity of 1,000 tonnes.

This milestone marks a significant step in developing robust power infrastructure for large-scale data centre operations. Power transformers like this LON01 unit play a crucial role in efficiently transferring electrical energy between different voltage levels, supporting reliable data centre activity. The use of a high-capacity gantry highlights the critical logistical and engineering efforts required for handling major substation components.

With this installation complete, Pure DC advances infrastructure to meet the ongoing demand for secure and stable power supply in one of the UK's key data centre hubs.
