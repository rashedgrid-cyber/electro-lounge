Upcoming Webinar to Examine Variable Shunt Reactors and Their Impact on Grid Voltage Stability

Wed, 14 Oct 2026 11:36:53 GMT

📅 Online learning; the supplied announcement does not identify a host country.

Reinhausen is hosting a webinar on Tuesday, October 27, at 2 pm CET, covering the basics of Variable Shunt Reactors, or VSRs.

A shunt reactor absorbs reactive power. Reactive power supports electric and magnetic fields in the network, and its balance affects voltage. A variable shunt reactor allows the amount absorbed to be adjusted, giving operators another way to respond as network conditions change.

The session will explore reactive power compensation and voltage control—useful subjects for anyone trying to connect equipment behaviour with day-to-day grid operation.

🔎 MW and MVAr describe different things. MW measures real power that does useful work; MVAr measures reactive power exchanged with electric and magnetic fields. Long high-voltage cables and lightly loaded lines can supply reactive power and raise voltage. A shunt reactor is connected in parallel with the network and absorbs reactive power to help control that rise. A variable reactor adjusts the amount absorbed as conditions change. It helps balance reactive power; it does not generate electricity or replace all other voltage-control equipment.
