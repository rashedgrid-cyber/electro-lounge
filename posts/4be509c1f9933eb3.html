Eleven Power Receives 25-Year Distribution Licence for Gurugram and Nuh, India

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 04:19:34 GMT

The Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC) has granted a 25-year electricity distribution licence to Eleven Power, authorizing operations specifically in Gurugram and Nuh, Haryana, India. This development directly impacts the ongoing transformation of power distribution in one of the country’s rapidly urbanizing regions.

Background and Regulatory Update Haryana, a state in northern India, is experiencing growing urbanization and increased demand for reliable electricity supply. Gurugram (often still referred to as Gurgaon) and Nuh are two key districts within Haryana targeted for new distribution infrastructure and services. The recent licence awarded by HERC to Eleven Power represents a long-term regulatory commitment, providing the company with a 25-year operational horizon to serve both commercial and residential customers.

About the Licence Grant A distribution licence, as conferred by HERC, gives the holder legal authority to distribute electricity to end users in a specific service area. This is essential for establishing new infrastructure, ensuring supply quality, and fostering competition where permitted. The 25-year term provides investment certainty, allowing Eleven Power to plan, develop, and maintain the necessary grid infrastructure, substations, and service lines in Gurugram and Nuh.

Implications for Electrical Professionals Electrical professionals in this region can expect an increase in project opportunities related to grid modernization, distribution network expansion, and end-user service improvements. The introduction of a new licensed distributor may support technological upgrades such as enhanced metering, improved network monitoring, and initiatives aimed at reducing distribution losses.

Grid Expansion and Urban Growth Newly awarded distribution licences typically motivate significant investment in physical and digital grid infrastructure. This may include installation of new overhead or underground lines, construction and upgrade of substations, implementation of advanced protection and control systems, and integration with renewable energy sources where applicable. Given the urban and peri-urban context of Gurugram and Nuh, solutions must address high load density, reliability requirements, and the evolving needs of commercial enterprises, residential communities, and municipal infrastructure.

The Role of Regulatory Oversight The approval and oversight by the Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission are central to promoting fair, reliable, and efficient distribution services. Regulatory monitoring ensures that Eleven Power must meet technical and customer service standards, including power quality, outage management, and responsiveness to consumer needs.

Looking Ahead The granting of a 25-year distribution licence to Eleven Power signals a new phase for electricity distribution in Gurugram and Nuh. Electrical professionals—from engineers and planners to contractors and grid operators—will play crucial roles as the new distributor establishes systems, upgrades infrastructure, and implements best practices in distribution network management over the coming decades.

This development underscores the importance of long-term regulatory support, investment in reliable infrastructure, and the growing role of modern distribution practices in one of India’s most dynamic urban areas. ⚡🏙️
