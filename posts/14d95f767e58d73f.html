Elecgene: Electrical Equipment Testing & Monitoring Solutions

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:34:18 GMT

Elecgene, a company of Xianheng International (Hangzhou) Electrical Manufacturing Co., Ltd., develops and manufactures electrical equipment testing and status monitoring devices. Established in October 2013, Elecgene focuses on the research, production, and sales of equipment for industries such as power, petroleum, petrochemicals, rail transit, and airports. Their product portfolio includes tools for visual inspection, insulation testing, power transformer testing, power protection, acoustic imaging, and high-voltage test systems.

Elecgene provides a range of instruments for system maintenance, equipment diagnosis, and power system integrity testing. Offerings include devices for relay protection, transformer analysis, circuit breaker testing, insulation assessment, and real-time monitoring of operational equipment. The company states that their solutions are used by utilities, manufacturers, and laboratories globally.

Potential users should verify product specifications, suitability, and compliance with local requirements directly with the manufacturer before making any purchasing decisions.
