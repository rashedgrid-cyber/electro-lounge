DG Matrix Launches 800 VDC Solid-State Transformer System for AI Data Centres

Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:07:35 GMT

🔌 San Jose, California, United States — DG Matrix introduced Interport 360 Flex at the OCP Global Summit held on October 12–15, 2026. The system is designed to convert medium- or low-voltage AC supplies into 800 VDC for AI data centres. The company states that AC inputs up to 34.5 kV are supported.

For beginners, AC changes direction periodically; DC flows in one direction. “800 VDC” describes the DC voltage, not the system's power capacity. A solid-state transformer uses controlled power-electronic conversion and electrical isolation to manage energy transfer. It should not be confused with a conventional transformer simply connected to a DC supply.

Inside the announced design, silicon-carbide semiconductor modules use liquid cooling. Modules are assembled into outdoor skids that can be connected in parallel as capacity grows. DG Matrix says its controls coordinate the conversion modules, storage and power sources. Storage is intended to help smooth rapid changes in AI workload demand.

The practical engineering issue is matching the supply system to a fast-changing load while controlling voltage and protecting equipment. The announcement describes an architecture and its stated functions; it does not establish independently measured efficiency, savings or performance at every installation.
