PT. Mastra Mulia Mandiri: Indonesian Manufacturer of Distribution Transformers

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 09:01:57 GMT

According to its website, PT. Mastra Mulia Mandiri manufactures MONTANA oil-immersed and dry-type distribution transformers in Indonesia. The claimed product range spans from 25 to 5,000 kVA, with designs built to IEC 60076 and SPLN standards. PT. Mastra Mulia Mandiri lists products such as pole-mounted CSP transformers (25–50 kVA), distribution transformers (25–2500 kVA), dry-type transformers (630–5000 kVA), medium power transformers (2500–5000 kVA), and trailer-mounted mobile substations (200–1000 kVA). The company states that all units undergo multi-stage quality control, in-house testing, and can be customized. Services described include commissioning, scheduled maintenance, refurbishment, gasket and oil work, and support via WhatsApp or email. PT. Mastra Mulia Mandiri’s website states the company is open to international procurement and export. For verified technical details, compliance certifications, and up-to-date product specifications, direct consultation with the company is advised.
