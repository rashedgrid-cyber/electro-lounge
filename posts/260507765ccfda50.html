HaiBian Power: Solutions in Switchgear, Energy Storage, and Transformers

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:34:18 GMT

HaiBian Power Equipment (HBPE) offers products in switchgear, energy storage, photovoltaic (PV) substations, and transformers. Product families include gas-insulated ring main switchgear (such as the HP-ELS-V Eco-Gas and HP-SRM SF6 units), LV and HV battery series, integrated energy storage systems, and various types of switchgear and transformers. According to the company's website, these products serve applications from C&I storage to smart substations and power transformers. The website outlines that eco-gas models use insulating media designed to avoid SF6 emissions, and solutions are tested to claim compliance with IEC, IEEE, and GB standards. The product range includes modular, sealed enclosures for dust and moisture protection, and embedded features like online monitoring for certain distribution units. HaiBian Power claims direct supply, worldwide export documentation, and support through technical and after-sales service channels. All technical specifications and suitability for specific applications should be independently verified using the latest official documentation and direct inquiries through the company's listed channels.
