Netel Secures $3.2M Contract for E.ON Substation Project in Sweden

Sun, 11 Oct 2026 09:32:24 GMT

🏗️ Bollstabruk, Sweden.

A new substation project is taking shape in Bollstabruk, Sweden. Netel has secured a $3.2 million contract with E.ON, with completion scheduled for 2029.

For the people working on projects like this, the interest goes beyond the contract value. Design, construction and operation all need to fit together, and decisions made early can affect how the installation is maintained later.

🔎 How the pieces fit: the electrical design defines the required ratings and equipment arrangement; construction turns that design into the physical installation; commissioning checks that the equipment and protection work together before service. Protection detects electrical faults and initiates isolation of the affected part. Coordination between these stages helps avoid installing equipment that is difficult to test, operate or maintain. These are general substation principles, not a description of this project's final design.
