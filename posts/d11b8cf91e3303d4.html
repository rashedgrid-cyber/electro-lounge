ALL-TEST Pro: Electric Motor Testing Equipment and Predictive Maintenance Solutions

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 09:01:57 GMT

ALL-TEST Pro provides electric motor testing equipment and predictive maintenance (PdM) tools for evaluating the condition of AC and DC motors, transformers, generators, and related systems. According to company statements, ALL-TEST Pro offers solutions that include handheld analyzers such as the AT7 Pro and ATPOL III, capable of diagnosing conditions like winding faults, ground faults, rotor issues, and contamination. Testing can be performed both deenergized (offline, MCA™) and energized (online, ESA) to support troubleshooting, condition monitoring, and installation verification across various industries.

ALL-TEST Pro claims its technology enables fast diagnostics and reporting, with products supporting advanced analysis including stator and rotor evaluation, power quality assessment, and mechanical diagnostics such as driven load issues and dynamic startup analysis. The company also provides additional services like onsite and online training, technical support, and upgrade options.

For those interested in current specifications and application suitability, users should consult the latest documentation and resources through the official ALL-TEST Pro website.
